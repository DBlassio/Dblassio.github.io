### What I've built

- Designed and published multimodal fusion architectures combining transformer language models (DeBERTa, mDeBERTa) with vision encoders (CLIP, ALIGN) that includes a gated fusion mechanism that learns *which* modality matters, per input, without post-hoc explanation. Published at **COMMA 2026** and **CLEF 2026**.
- Built and deployed production ML systems at Marsh McLennan: anomaly detection, time-series forecasting, FastAPI + Docker pipelines running at enterprise scale.
- Spent the past two years designing architectures that combine text, vision, and physiological signals (EEG, eye-tracking). Trying to get AI a little closer to how humans actually perceive things.
