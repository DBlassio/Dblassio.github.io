<div style='text-align: justify;'>
I am Diego - a Machine Learning Engineer and a researcher in NeuroAI who is motivated by a simple idea: Understanding how the brain works will help us to create better and intelligent AI. My work includes business-oriented ML systems, fundamental AI research, and projects that sit at the intersection between neuroscience and deep learning. I see AI as a tool to understand human cognition and accelerate the decision-making process for developing scalable and interpretable products that have real-world impact.<br/><br/></div>

<div style='text-align: justify;'>
Technically, I work with Python, R, PyTorch, Hugging Face, scikit-learn. My projects have been involved in anomaly detection, time-series forecasting, clustering techniques, NLP, computer vision and multimodal models. Working closely with MLOps has really influenced how I approach design: reproducible, scalable and interpretable. This is why I enjoy keeping my pipelines clean (DVC, Git), creating deployment frameworks (FastAPI, Docker) and using practices that allow research to be transferred to production.<br/><br/></div>

<div style='text-align: justify;'>

I love turning complex results into straightforward insights. When I’m not building models, I am outside: either hiking or training endurance sports: (trail)running, cycling, swimming.<br/><br/></div>
