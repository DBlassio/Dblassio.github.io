I'm Diego — a Multimodal AI Engineer and Researcher working on a 
simple bet: **if we understand how the brain processes information, 
we can build AI that's smarter, more interpretable, and more 
human-aligned.**

I move between two worlds that don't usually talk to each other: 
applied industry ML and academic AI research. Both have shaped how 
I build things.

### What I've built

- Designed and published multimodal fusion architectures combining 
  transformer language models (DeBERTa, mDeBERTa) with vision 
  encoders (CLIP, ALIGN) — including a gated fusion mechanism that 
  learns *which* modality matters, per input, without post-hoc 
  explanation. Published at **COMMA 2026** and **CLEF 2026**.
- Built and deployed production ML systems at Marsh McLennan — 
  anomaly detection, time-series forecasting, FastAPI + Docker 
  pipelines running at enterprise scale.
- Spent the past two years designing architectures that combine 
  text, vision, and physiological signals (EEG, eye-tracking) — 
  trying to get AI a little closer to how humans actually perceive 
  things.

### What I'm building now

I'm currently deep in **retrieval-augmented generation and applied 
GenAI** — extending the same multimodal fusion principles from my 
research into production RAG systems, working toward AWS 
certification, and figuring out how to make research-grade ideas 
ship reliably.
